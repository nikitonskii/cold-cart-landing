How do I save an item?
Copy the product's link from any online store and paste it into Cold Cart's "Add to Cart" screen — or use your browser's Share sheet and send it straight to Cold Cart. We'll pull in the title, price, and image automatically, and start the 48-hour cool-down.
Why is the price or image missing?
We read those details from the store's public product page. Some sites block automated reads, hide prices behind a login, or lay out their pages in a way we can't parse. When that happens, the item is still saved — you can simply type the name and price in yourself, and the cool-down works exactly the same.
How does the 48-hour reminder work?
The moment you add an item, a 48-hour timer starts. When it reaches zero, Cold Cart sends you a push notification so you can make the call with a clear head. Make sure notifications are enabled for Cold Cart in your phone's settings — without them, the timer still runs but you won't get the nudge.
How do I delete my account?
Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your account, saved items, uploaded photos, and preferences from our servers. Prefer we do it for you? Email curiosity.applications@gmail.com and we'll handle it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

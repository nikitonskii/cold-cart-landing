What it does

Built to slow the moment
between want and buy

Cold Cart isn't another wishlist. It's a deliberate pause that turns impulse into intention.

The cool-down timer

Add something you want and a 48-hour timer starts ticking. You can't check out — you can only wait. When the timer ends, the rush is usually gone, and the real decision begins.

23:59left to decide

Save the link, not the impulse

Paste any product URL — Amazon, Zalando, your favourite boutique — and Cold Cart keeps the name, price and link safe while the heat fades.

Watch your savings grow

Every "I don't need it" adds to your streak. Cold Cart tallies what you've chosen not to buy — restraint you can actually see.

Wishlists you can share

Cold Cart isn't just for cooling off — it's where you collect everything you love. Group items into wishlists and share them with the people you trust, so you can plan a trip, build a gift list, or co-curate a home together, with everyone's picks in one place.

How it works

Three steps to a calmer cart

No willpower required — just a little time on your side.

01

Drop the link

Found something tempting? Paste the link or add it straight from your browser's share sheet. It takes seconds.

02

Let it cool

The item sits in your Cold Cart for 48 hours. No buying, no second-guessing — just a little breathing room.

03

Decide clearly

When the timer ends, choose: buy it with intention, or let it go and bank the savings to your streak.

Good to know

Questions, answered

When can I get Cold Cart?
Cold Cart is in the final stretch before launch. It's coming soon to both the App Store and Google Play. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know the moment it's live.
How does the 48-hour timer work?
The second you add an item, a 48-hour countdown begins. During that window the item simply waits in your Cold Cart — you can review it, but the decision to buy is intentionally paused. When the timer reaches zero, you choose whether to go ahead or let it go.
Which stores does it work with?
Any of them. Cold Cart works with a product link, so you can save items from virtually any online shop — paste a URL or use your browser's share sheet to send it straight to your cart.
Does Cold Cart cost anything?
We'll share full pricing details closer to launch. Waitlist members will be the first to know — and the first to try it.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes, any time. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data from within the app or by email at any point.
